Sectors

The four sectors the stokvel invests in.

Logistics

Trucks, fleets and freight services that keep products moving between farms, mines and markets.

  • Freight and haulage fleets
  • Cold-chain transport
  • Brokerage and routing services

Commercial agriculture

Large-scale crop and livestock production that supplies food and export markets.

  • Grain, soya and citrus production
  • Livestock operations
  • Packing and processing

Mining

Stakes in mining operations and the services that support them.

  • Mining services contracts
  • Equipment and supply partnerships
  • Processing and transport links

Distribution centres

Warehouses that store and ship goods for retailers and producers.

  • Warehouse ownership and leasing
  • Storage and fulfilment
  • Expansion of existing sites
