Privacy notice

What we store and why.

What is stored

  • Your user name (visible to other members on the dashboard)
  • A hash of your password — never the password itself
  • The investments you record: sector, amount, date

Who can see it

All signed-in members see pooled totals and each member’s investment per sector. Passwords are never visible to anyone.

Where it is stored

On the server hosting this site, in a private SQLite database. Not shared with third parties.

Your choices

You can correct or remove your own investment entries from the dashboard. To close your account, message the committee in the WhatsApp group.

Cookies

No cookies. Your session token lives in your browser’s local storage and is cleared on sign-out.
