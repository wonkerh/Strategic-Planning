How to join

Joining takes a few minutes and starts on WhatsApp.

  1. Join the WhatsApp groupTap the join button and introduce yourself to the committee.
  2. Pay your monthly contributionContribute the agreed amount by the due date.
  3. The group investsMembers vote on which sector receives the pooled funds.
  4. Track it on the dashboardSee the pool, sector allocation and every investment.

Common questions

How much is the monthly contribution?

The committee confirms the amount in the WhatsApp group when you join.

Who decides where the money goes?

Members vote on investments, guided by the committee.

Can I lose money?

Yes. All investments carry risk and returns are not guaranteed.

How do I see my investment?

Open the dashboard to see the pool, sector allocation, investments and recent contributions.
